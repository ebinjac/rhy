An **application** is Rhythm's trusted ownership boundary. It contains a name, owner, environment, CAR ID, services, linked monitors, ELF queries, and OpenSearch alert receivers.

## CAR ID

A CAR ID is the internal identifier your organization uses to recognize an application. It is metadata, not a secret or an authentication credential.

## Services

Services divide an application into deployable or operational components. A service can override its application's default ELF index pattern, time field, and semantic log-field mapping.

## Trusted ownership

Webhook payloads cannot move an alert to another application. The receiver's application and optional service assignment determine ownership.

<SecurityNote>
Use application masking rules for sensitive log fields. Service overrides may narrow an index pattern, but they cannot bypass the global ELF host and index allowlists.
</SecurityNote>
