The baseline window ends exactly at deployment start. Supported windows are 24 hours, 7 days, and 30 days.

Only successful, measured runs from the same published revision enter latency distributions. At least five samples are required. Insufficient history warns but does not block.

The baseline preview lists eligible samples per monitor, revision compatibility, exclusions, and estimated post-deployment load before execution starts.
