Check connection health and the resolved application/service index before editing the query.

- **Authentication or authorization**: verify the referenced credential and search permission.
- **Index**: confirm resolution precedence and the allowed-index policy.
- **Syntax or policy**: use Validate to locate unsupported Query DSL.
- **Timeout**: narrow the time range or reduce query complexity.
- **Partial shards**: Rhythm rejects partial search results for gates.
- **Response limit**: reduce displayed fields, hits, or aggregation buckets.

Debug evidence contains the compiled body and a redacted cURL command, not unrestricted credentials or raw sensitive documents.
